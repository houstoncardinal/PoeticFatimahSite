# Poetically Fatimah — Website Blueprint

> Goal: Launch a powerful, high‑end personal brand site that showcases Fatimah’s poetry, voice, events, and products, while capturing emails, bookings, and sales.

---

## 1) Brand Foundation

**Brand essence:** Soulful, empowering, reflective, healing, bold.

**Voice & tone:** Intimate, poetic, confident, warm. Short melodic lines, sensory phrasing, grounded calls‑to‑action.

**Audience:** Poetry lovers, event organizers, educators, brand collabs, podcast hosts, female empowerment & wellness communities.

**One‑line value prop:** “Poetry that heals, words that move rooms, and performances that stay with you.”

**Tagline ideas:**

* “Where words find their wings.”
* “Poetry you can feel.”
* “Soft power. Strong voice.”

**Color palette (suggested):**

* Obsidian Black (#0E0E10)
* Porcelain White (#FAFAFA)
* Rose Gold (#B76E79)
* Deep Plum (#4C2A4E)
* Soft Sand (#E8DCCB)

**Type system:**

* Display: **Playfair Display** or **Cormorant Garamond** (elegant, editorial)
* Body/UI: **Inter** or **General Sans** (modern, accessible)

**Imagery art‑direction:** Cinematic portraits, soft natural light, warm grain, minimal clutter. Handwritten accents (SVG brush strokes). B‑roll: hands writing, stage silhouettes, pages turning.

---

## 2) Site Architecture (Sitemap)

1. **Home** (Hero, highlight poem, performance reel, newsletter, CTAs)
2. **Poetry** (Collections + single poem pages; audio read‑aloud)
3. **Performances** (Reel, past shows, speaker packet/EPK, booking CTA)
4. **Shop** (Books, prints, digital downloads, limited merch)
5. **Events** (Calendar + RSVP/subscribe)
6. **About** (Artist bio, mission, press kit)
7. **Contact / Booking** (Form + email capture; press & collab)
8. **Journal** (short posts: behind‑the‑scenes, drafts, reflections)

*Utility:* Privacy, Terms, 404 (with poem fragment), Link-in-bio page.

## 4) Poetry Section (Structure & UX)

* Within each collection: poem cards → single poem page.

**Poem page micro‑copy (template):**

## 5) Performances / EPK

**Reel at top**, then sections:

* **Topics/Themes:** Healing, identity, love, womanhood, faith, resilience.

## 11) Accessibility & Performance

* Large contrast, 16–18px base, 1.6–1.8 line-height.
* Keyboard focus states, aria‑labels on audio players, transcripts for reels.
* Lazy‑load media; serve WebP; preconnect fonts; cache IG feed server‑side.

---

## 12) Integrations

* **Email**: ConvertKit or Beehiiv (lead magnet: “7 Poems to Keep Close”).
* **Analytics**: Plausible or GA4; Hotjar for heatmaps (first month only).
* **Forms**: Tally or Typeform → email + Notion/Airtable sync.
* **CMS**: Notion (fast) or Sanity (scalable).
* **Search**: Algolia Lite or client‑side Fuse.js (for poems).

## 18) Lovable.dev Build Prompt (Paste‑Ready)

**Title:** Poetically Fatimah — Premium Poetry & Performance Site

**High‑level brief:**
Build a sleek, editorial‑grade website for a poet/performer named Fatimah (brand: “Poetically Fatimah”). Prioritize fast load, accessibility, elegant typography, and cinematic imagery. Pages: Home, Poetry (collections + single poem), Performances/EPK, Shop (embed), Events, About, Journal, Contact/Booking, Link‑in‑bio. Integrations: ConvertKit, Gumroad/Shopify Lite, Tally forms, Plausible analytics. Include JSON‑LD for Person, CreativeWork (poems), Event, and Product when applicable.

**Functional requirements:**

* Global header/footer, mobile nav drawer.
* Home hero with 3 headline variants + subhead + dual CTAs.
* Spotlight Poem component with audio player + transcript toggle.
* Performances page with embedded reel, topic tags, EPK download.
* Shop page using embeds (Gumroad or Shopify Lite buttons).
* Events page with simple CMS (title, date, city, link) and past events archive.
* Poetry collections grid; single poem page with audio upload + transcript field.
* Newsletter signup (ConvertKit API) with success state + double opt‑in text.
* Contact/Booking form (Tally/Typeform embed) and a plain email fallback.
* Link‑in‑bio page with buttons and simple analytics.
* SEO basics (meta tags, OpenGraph), sitemap.xml, robots.txt.
* Accessibility pass (aria, focus states, sufficient contrast).

**Design direction:**

* Palette: Obsidian/Porcelain/Rose‑Gold/Plum/Sand; lots of whitespace; oversized quotes.
* Typography: Playfair Display for headlines, Inter for body.
* Components: Cards, large audio player, quote blocks, soft shadows, 2xl radius.
* Micro‑animations on hover/scroll; lazy‑load images/video; subtle grain texture.

**CMS schema:**

* Poem: {title, slug, body (rich text), audioUrl, collection, themes[], excerpt, createdAt}
* Collection: {title, slug, description}
* Event: {title, date, city, venue, link, isPast}
* Testimonial: {quote, name, role, source}
* ProductStub: {title, blurb, priceText, buyUrl, image}

**Deliverables:**

* Fully responsive site with the above pages and components.
* Admin-friendly CMS or content JSON files for quick edits.
* Deploy on Vercel/Netlify with environment variables for APIs.
